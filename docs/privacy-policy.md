# Ritune Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Ritune is a daily-rhythm app for Android. It is built to work entirely on your device.

## What Ritune collects

Nothing. Ritune does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. The app does not request internet access, has no account system, and contains no advertising or analytics.

## What stays on your device

The anchors you log, your routines, your run history and your settings are stored locally on your phone. They are never sent to the developer or to any third party by the app.

## Backup

Ritune allows Android's backup service to include this local data. If you have Google backup turned on, Android may store an encrypted copy in your Google account and restore it when you set up a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google under your device settings and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), not by Ritune. You can turn it off in your device's system settings.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: show the ongoing notification and controls while a routine is running, and a notification when it finishes.
- **Foreground service**: keep a routine's timer and cues running while the screen is off.
- **Wake lock**: let step timers fire on time while a routine is running.
- **Vibration**: haptic cues at each step.

Spoken cues use the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device. Ritune does not record audio.

## Deleting your data

In Settings, "Delete all data" removes everything Ritune has stored. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

## Children

Ritune does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Health notice

Ritune is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Consult a professional before starting a new exercise or sleep routine.
