# Ritune Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

Ritune is a daily-rhythm app for Android. Everything you log stays on your device. The one exception is the optional rhythm coach, which sends data only when you turn it on and ask it a question.

## What Ritune collects

The developer does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Ritune has no account system, no advertising and no analytics.

If you use the rhythm coach, your questions and some of your data are sent to Google to get an answer. That is described below.

## What stays on your device

The anchors you log, your routines, your run history and your settings are stored on your phone. Coach conversations are kept in memory only and are gone when you leave the coach screen.

## The rhythm coach

The coach is off until you turn it on. You can turn it off any time in Settings, and nothing is sent while it is off.

When you ask a question, Ritune sends it to Google's Gemini model through Google Firebase. To answer, the coach also sends the parts of your data it needs: your anchors and their target times, the times you logged them, your routines and your run history. It does not send your name, contacts, location or anything else from your phone.

To protect the service from misuse, Ritune also uses:

- **Firebase Authentication**, which gives this install an anonymous ID so the number of questions each install sends can be limited. The ID is not linked to your name, email or Google account. Turning the coach off deletes it.
- **Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity**, which lets Google confirm the request comes from the real Ritune app installed from Google Play.

Google processes this data under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), the [Firebase privacy terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and the [Gemini API Additional Terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms). Ritune uses the Gemini API's free tier, so Google may use your requests and the answers to improve its services, and people may review them. Don't type anything into the coach that you wouldn't want shared.

The coach can suggest changes, such as a new target time, a new routine or a fix to a log. Nothing changes unless you tap Apply, and the change is made on your device.

## Backup

Ritune allows Android's backup service to include its local data. If you have Google backup turned on, Android may store an encrypted copy in your Google account and restore it when you set up a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google under your device settings and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), not by Ritune. You can turn it off in your device's system settings.

## Permissions

- **Internet**: used only by the rhythm coach, when it is turned on.
- **Notifications**: show the ongoing notification and controls while a routine is running, and a notification when it finishes.
- **Foreground service**: keep a routine's timer and cues running while the screen is off.
- **Wake lock**: let step timers fire on time while a routine is running.
- **Vibration**: haptic cues at each step.

Spoken cues use the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device. Ritune does not record audio.

## Deleting your data

In Settings, "Delete all data" removes everything Ritune has stored on your device and deletes the coach's anonymous ID. Uninstalling the app also removes the data on your device.

Questions you sent to the coach are held by Google under the terms linked above. Ritune cannot see or delete them.

## Children

Ritune is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Health notice

Ritune and its coach are not a medical device and do not give medical advice. The coach can make mistakes. Consult a professional before starting a new exercise or sleep routine.
